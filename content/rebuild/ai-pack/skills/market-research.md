# Market research

## When to use
- Before the first campaign for a new offer or a new type of customer.
- When replies slow down and you want a new angle.

## Ask the owner first
Ask these four questions and wait for the answers:
1. Who do you want to meet? (company type and job title)
2. What is one good customer worth to you in the first year?
3. How long does a good buyer take to decide?
4. Which company types should we never contact?

Repeat the answers back in three lines and ask the owner to confirm.

## Steps
1. Search the web for recent events that push these buyers to spend money now. Look back 6 to 12 months and ahead to upcoming deadlines. Good places to look: new rules and compliance deadlines, funding or grant programs, tariff and price changes, new plants, sites and expansions, big hiring pushes, industry trade shows, supply problems, technology changes.
2. For each event, write down who has to act, what they have to buy, and roughly how many companies it touches. Keep the 3 to 5 events where the owner sells what those buyers now need. These are the triggers.
3. Collect 10 real buyer quotes about the problem the owner solves. Use forums, reviews, trade press, interviews and public job posts. Copy the words exactly and keep the link.
4. List the job titles that buy and the titles that can say yes.
5. List the company types to skip: the owner's list, plus any you found, such as companies too small to afford the offer.
6. Note where ready-made lists of these companies exist: association member lists, trade show exhibitor lists, licence or certification registries, public filings. The signal-list-builder skill uses these.
7. Save research-brief.md with these sections: Owner answers, Triggers (event, date, source link, who must act, what they buy), Buyer quotes (quote and link), Buying titles, Skip list, Where to find the companies, Open questions.

## Rules
- Every trigger and every quote has a source link. Anything without one is marked UNKNOWN.
- Never invent a quote, a number or an event.
- Write in plain words. The owner is new to this.
- Research only. Do not write emails in this skill.

## What good output looks like
- 3 to 5 dated triggers, each with a link and a clear line on who must buy what.
- 10 buyer quotes in the buyers' own words, each with a link.
- A titles list and a skip list the owner has approved.
- At least one place where a ready-made company list exists.

## Authorization and privacy

Store credentials through secure connections and keep keys, tokens and account identifiers out of files and reports.
