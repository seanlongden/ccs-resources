# Prepare A Client Profile With AI
Pack: 0.2.0-preview. Additional instruction created to fill the runbook's missing client-onboarding reference.

Read the completed onboarding form and signed-off scope. Extract service, delivery capability, buyer, exclusions, geography, proof, customer value/period, capacity, sales cycle, responsibilities and approval owner into profile.md. Trace each fact to the supplied answer. Leave missing facts UNKNOWN. Do not infer a margin, customer result or account permission. Separate agency acquisition from client delivery. Return the profile, unresolved questions and any scope mismatch for owner review. Do not send, book, purchase or change accounts.

Completion: the owner checks each value against the form and agrees the context. The skill is not claimed installed or tested inside Grok; it is a reviewable written instruction.
