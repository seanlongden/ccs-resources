## Calculations
Customers = results × close rate.
Stated-period revenue = customers × customer revenue value.
Revenue multiple = stated-period revenue ÷ your fee.
Fee share = your fee ÷ stated-period revenue × 100.
If there are zero sales, revenue is zero and the multiple is zero. If fee is zero, do not divide by it.
These are revenue comparisons, not buyer profit or guaranteed returns.
