# Research Brief
Pack: 0.2.0-preview

## Owner-confirmed Facts
Who buys, who benefits, service, customer value and period, sales cycle, capacity, exclusions.

## Sources
For each: URL, access date, exact observation or short quote, why it matters, confidence, what it does NOT prove.

## Market Triggers
Event, date, affected buyer, what they may need, source and timing. Unsourced = UNKNOWN. A trigger is targeting evidence, not automatic permission to mention it in copy.

## Buyer Language
Use sourced public quotes or approved customer interviews. Do not turn your interpretation into a quotation.

## Buying Titles And Counterparties
Separate agency customers from their own customers.

## Lead Sources
Named source, filter recipe, date, estimated count and limitations. A count is not verified until the search is run.

## Open Questions
Unknown economics, capacity, permissions, legal or technical access. Stop where these block the next action.
