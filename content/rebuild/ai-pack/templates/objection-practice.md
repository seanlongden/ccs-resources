# Objection Practice
Pack: 1.0.0. Role-plays are examples, not verbatim historical calls.

For each concern record the actual words, context, question, evidence, answer, permitted terms and next action.
1. Too expensive: ask whether value, budget, risk or cash timing is the issue.
2. I can do it myself: explain actual work handled and ask what they want to retain.
3. No relevant case study: be honest; show a real process demonstration with limits.
4. Want performance only: assess costs, attribution and a defined minimum; do not concede blindly.
5. Need partner approval: identify the question and provide a forwardable scope.
6. Need more time: agree a real decision step or close the loop respectfully.
7. How does it work: explain data, list, copy, replies and client role as actually scoped.
8. Can you guarantee sales: distinguish the controllable opportunity from the client's final close.
