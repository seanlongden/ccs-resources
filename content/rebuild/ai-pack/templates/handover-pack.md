# Student Handover Pack
Pack: 1.0.0

Status: NOT READY until all required evidence is real and reviewed.

1. Business/context/owner, version and date.
2. What is installed, tools/plans, secure connection names and account owner.
3. Approved offer, buyer rules, proof, copy and campaign versions.
4. Campaign identifiers, current status, sender group and settings.
5. Counts: raw, rejected, held, qualified, accepted upload; reconcile differences.
6. Demonstration evidence: research reviewed; rendered previews checked; first qualified reply; correct booking; stop-on-reply; reporting. Link dated evidence. Label simulation.
7. Active routines: owner, trigger, time zone, input, output, stop rule, last successful run, failure alert. None active in preview.
8. Daily work: review inbox, fit cards, exact approvals and bookings.
9. Weekly work: health, results, runway, refill, next copy test.
10. Known problems, workarounds, blocked permissions and who resolves them.
11. Next seven days: dated actions and responsible people.
12. Student demonstration and CCS review: show how to find a failed run, hold a bad lead and approve a draft. Reviewer/date/decision.

A successful mock booking is not a live result. A single live booking proves a working chain, not repeatable profitability. Do not reinstall the same skills as a handover step.
