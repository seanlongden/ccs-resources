# Seven Deal Structures
Pack: 0.2.0-preview. These are choices to assess, not seven recommended promises.

| Structure | Collect For | Main Question |
|---|---|---|
| Retainer | Agreed work each month | Does the fee cover the whole monthly scope? |
| Per Lead | A defined interested lead | What evidence makes the lead billable? |
| Per Call | Booked or attended call as agreed | How are duplicates, cancellations and no-shows treated? |
| One-Time Build Plus Operation | Build once, then manage | What ends after build and what repeats? |
| Quarterly Engagement | Defined work/result over 90 days | What is target versus guaranteed minimum? |
| Milestone Split | Initial build and accepted delivery stages | What evidence triggers each payment? |
| Engagement Plus Performance/Revenue Share | Base work plus attributed result | Who supplies reliable sales data and when is payment due? |
