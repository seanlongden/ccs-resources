# Proposal Template
Pack: 0.2.0-preview. Adapted from CCS S4.

## Abstract
We discussed [current situation] and [desired result]. We propose [specific work and opportunity milestone] over [term], for [agreed budget and payment shape].

## Your Situation
Use their actual words, current route, constraint and capacity. Unknown facts stay UNKNOWN.

## What We Will Build And Operate
Research, data sources, lists, campaign writing, testing, reply work and reporting. State who does each. Separate an installed system from ongoing management.

## Who We Will Reach
Company types, titles, geography, exclusions and evidence of a reachable market. Label estimates; never invent a verified list size.

## What Counts
The buyer's own opportunity milestone. Include clear interest, ability to buy, meaningful size and service scope. Describe evidence, rejection and dispute handling.

## What To Expect
Conservative steps from new contacts to interest, meetings and accepted opportunities, with source and denominator. No invented conversion rate. Revenue comparisons use their stated values and periods, not assumed profit.

## Client Responsibilities
Named contact, timely replies, attendance, feedback, accurate proof and delivery capacity.

## Terms
Price/currency/tax, term/start date, first payment, later payment trigger, approved minimum, guarantee clock and remedy, required participation, exclusions. No blank commercial terms in a final proposal.

## Next Steps
Review date with decision-makers, requested feedback, agreement and payment before kickoff.

## Note To Decision-makers
Summarise the problem, route, investment, work handled, client role and protection in 3 to 5 sentences.

Drafting instruction: Read approved discovery notes, offer.md and proof. Extract exact stated numbers and concerns with transcript references. Draft this structure. Flag anything not agreed; do not invent a price, guarantee or result. Return a missing-facts list and stop for review. Do not send.
