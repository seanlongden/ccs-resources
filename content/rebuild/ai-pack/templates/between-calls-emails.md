# Five Emails Between Discovery And Decision
Pack: 0.2.0-preview. Adapted from CCS S3. Templates only, no sending authorised.

## 1. Same-day Continuity
Subject: Next steps

[Name], good speaking today. We have what we need to put together a scope around [their goal]. I'll send it by [day]. We have [day, time, zone] set to review it. If there is any context I missed, send it over.

## 2. Proposal Delivery
Subject: [Company] scope

[Name], here's the scope based on our call: [link]. It's a first draft, so please mark anything that does not match how you work. I'd most like your view on [one section] before [review date].

## 3. Before The Review, No Feedback Yet
[Name], we're on for [day, time, zone]. Did you get a chance to review [section]? Send any changes before [date] and I'll have the revised version ready.

## 4. Terms Agreed
[Name], thanks for today. Here is the agreement reflecting the terms we agreed, and the first invoice for [amount and currency]. Once that is complete, we'll arrange kickoff for [week].

## 5. Decision Request
[Name], checking in on the scope. I'm trying to get a yes or no so I do not keep bothering you. Is this still a priority for this quarter?

One ask per email. Use the same sales thread. Review recipient, attachments and terms yourself. If the prospect changes a fact, correct the proposal before sending anything further.
