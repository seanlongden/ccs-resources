# Voice and reply guide
Pack: 0.1.0-preview

Add 3 to 5 of your actual replies, with private details removed. Explain what you like about each. Record corrections as rules with a date.

Reply card: who, company and role; fit and reason; campaign/offer; our last email; full latest inbound; draft; real available times with zone; approval required.

GOOD: draft a direct answer and times. UNSURE: show the doubt for review. BAD: no draft. Opt-out: stop contact, no persuasive reply. Out of office: note return date; no automatic response. A referral is a new recipient and needs approval. Do not promise a different price or service in a reply.

Before any future send: re-read latest thread, sender, recipient, CC, approved copy, links and booking state. Any change invalidates the approval.
