# Email Pattern Library
Pack: 1.0.0. Rewritten from the existing 28-framework library. These are adaptable shapes, not independently verified winning campaigns or proof for your business. The current two-step rules and approved facts govern every use. Start with the three shapes taught in the lesson; do not use all 28 in one campaign.

## 1. Plain Connection Offer

Use only a result your service actually supports.

~~~text
We help [buyer] reach [their customer] for [useful result]. [One sentence of actual work]. Open to an intro?
~~~

## 2. Educational Invitation

The event and resource must exist.

~~~text
We are running [real session] about [useful task]. You will leave with [actual resource]. Would you like the details?
~~~

## 3. Evidence-Led Problem

No invented industry statistics.

~~~text
[Sourced fact with date]. We help [buyer] address [specific consequence] through [actual work]. Worth a brief intro?
~~~

## 4. Dated Trigger

Save the event/date/source, and check relevance.

~~~text
How are you handling [real event's relevant consequence]? We [actual offer]. Open to an intro?
~~~

## 5. Named Case

Requires actual evidence and permission.

~~~text
We helped [permitted client] achieve [verified result] in [period] through [work]. Is that relevant to [their goal]?
~~~

## 6. Anonymous Case

Anonymising does not permit an invented case.

~~~text
We helped [truthful company type] with [verified result/period]. We would use [relevant process] for [buyer]. Worth discussing?
~~~

## 7. Useful Playbook

Asset must be useful without buying.

~~~text
We put together [actual practical asset] for [buyer/task]. Would you like it?
~~~

## 8. Tailored Strategy

Only say prepared after the work exists.

~~~text
I prepared [actual checked strategy] for [specific buyer situation]. Would you like to see it?
~~~

## 9. Changing Provider

Do not assume an incumbent or invent a buyout.

~~~text
If you are reviewing [service], we handle [actual migration/work] under [real terms]. Would it be useful to compare?
~~~

## 10. Provider Concern Plus Proof

No unsupported claim about their current supplier.

~~~text
If [specific issue] is affecting your team, our [verified case or process] may be relevant. Worth an intro?
~~~

## 11. Direct Question

The answer should affect whether your offer fits.

~~~text
How are you currently [specific task]? We [actual useful work]. Worth a brief intro?
~~~

## 12. Completed Build Plus Operation

Distinguish build from management.

~~~text
We build [specific asset] and run [actual continuing tasks] so [buyer outcome]. Would you like the outline?
~~~

## 13. Performance Terms

Include base fees honestly; do not imply free risk.

~~~text
We provide [defined result] under [actual fee/trigger]. Would it be useful to see what counts?
~~~

## 14. AI-Assisted Work

No labour-saving number without evidence.

~~~text
Our system [specific AI task] with [human check] so your team [useful output]. Open to an intro?
~~~

## 15. Existing Demonstration

A generic demo is not a bespoke completed client system.

~~~text
I built [real demonstration] showing [specific task]. Would you like to see it?
~~~

## 16. Three Campaign Ideas

Each idea must fit your delivery.

~~~text
We could help with [outcome one], [outcome two] and [outcome three] through [actual work]. Worth an intro?
~~~

## 17. Research Insight

Keep sample limits and source.

~~~text
Our review of [actual source/sample] found [supported observation]. We can help with [relevant work]. Worth discussing?
~~~

## 18. Repurposing Content

Do not promise customers from outputs alone.

~~~text
We turn [client's available material] into [specific outputs] and handle [real distribution work]. Want the outline?
~~~

## 19. News Or Policy

Verify current status before use.

~~~text
[Verified dated change] affects [specific buyer]. We help with [actual response]. Worth an intro?
~~~

## 20. Seasonal Window

No invented deadline or scarcity.

~~~text
Before [real seasonal event/date], we can [specific work] for [buyer]. Would it be useful to discuss?
~~~

## 21. Specific Gap

A gap must be observable and relevant, not a guessed loss.

~~~text
I noticed [verified observable gap]. We [actual work to address it]. Want to see an example?
~~~

## 22. Visual Example

No pretending a suggested design is measured uplift.

~~~text
I made [actual mock-up] showing [specific improvement]. Would you like to see it?
~~~

## 23. Pilot

State paid versus free clearly.

~~~text
We can run [defined limited pilot] for [actual fee/term] with [completion test]. Want the scope?
~~~

## 24. Reachable Market

A count is not demand or exclusive access.

~~~text
We identified [verified count/source/date] matching [rules]. We can [actual offer] to that market. Worth discussing?
~~~

## 25. Public Activity

No fake engagement or implied familiarity.

~~~text
Your [real public point] raised [relevant question]. We [actual helpful offer]. Open to an intro?
~~~

## 26. Right Person Bump

Use as the one approved cold bump, not a new multi-step chain.

~~~text
[First name], are you the right person to ask about [actual offer]?
~~~

## 27. Simple Bump

One short same-thread step two.

~~~text
[First name], let me know if I can send the outline over.
~~~

## 28. Close The Loop

Alternative final bump, not an added third cold step.

~~~text
[First name], should I close the loop on this for now?
~~~

## Adapt One Pattern
Read my approved offer and proof. Choose one relevant pattern. Replace bracketed inputs with supported facts. If a fact is missing, flag it rather than invent it. Show one draft and wait for my review. Do not load or send.
