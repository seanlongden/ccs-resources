# Discovery script and notes
Pack: 0.1.0-preview. Adapted from CCS S2.

## Before the call
Read website and proof. Note what they sell, to whom, current sales method and a sourced reason they may need capacity. Do not ask things you can see on their site. Prepare a price range you can justify, not a surprise final number.

## Opening
“I had a look at your business before we spoke. It looks like you [specific observed fact]. Tell me what you are focused on now and what you would like the next 90 days to look like. Then we can see whether we can help.”

## Walk through their process
1. When someone first shows interest, what happens?
2. Who takes that first conversation?
3. What happens next? When do you decide it is worth pursuing?
4. What do you send them? Who else gets involved?
5. How many of these turn into paying customers?
6. What is one customer worth, over what period? What profit is actually known?
7. How long did the last few sales take?
8. How much extra work could the team fulfil?
9. What do you call a qualified opportunity internally?
10. Who should attend the proposal review?

## Close
Explain the proposed buyer, route and scope in 2 to 3 sentences. Give the actual range and payment shape. Book a separate review with decision-makers. Confirm when the proposal will arrive. Do not invent a target volume because the call is going well.

## Notes to complete
Their own words / process steps / opportunity definition / value and period / close rate and denominator / sales cycle / capacity / concerns / decision-makers / stated range / review date / unknowns.
