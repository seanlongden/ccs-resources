## The Two Audiences
The agency sells cold-email acquisition to maintenance-company owners. A maintenance company's delivery campaign reaches manufacturing plant or maintenance managers. These contexts need different profiles, proof and calendars.

## Three Buyer Groups Considered
1. Maintenance firms serving manufacturers: selected for this exercise because their service and business buyers are visible in the sources.
2. General facilities management: held because it can include low-value, consumer or public-bid work requiring tighter scope.
3. Machine parts suppliers: held because repeat small orders may not support this example fee without confirmed account economics.

## Three Angles To Investigate
- Planned shutdown maintenance: find a dated, public shutdown or project and confirm the need. The phrase alone is not an event.
- New equipment or plant expansion: confirm date, location and whether external maintenance is relevant. No live event is asserted here.
- Preventive maintenance: ask about an existing programme and supplier-review timing. Do not claim their current supplier is failing.

## What The Evidence Does Not Establish
No company-specific live trigger, reachable provider count, spare capacity, contract price, close rate or willingness to hire our agency has been verified. $60,000 customer revenue and the proposal terms are explicitly supplied exercise assumptions. A real launch must fill these gaps through research and discovery.

## How The Exercise Proceeds
Use the separate synthetic company rows to test cleaning, scoring and handoffs. Use the complete illustrative discovery notes to draft the example offer. This demonstrates the process without pretending public market research proves actual demand.
