# Research example
Pack: 0.1.0-preview. Checked 6 November 2026. Limited public research, not a completed live targeting approval.

## What real sources support
- https://www.advancedtech.com/ describes industrial maintenance, technology and parts services for manufacturers. This supports the distinction between a maintenance company and its manufacturing customers. It does not establish that ATS is a suitable prospect, has spare capacity or wants our service.
- https://www.bls.gov/ooh/installation-maintenance-and-repair/industrial-machinery-mechanics-and-maintenance-workers-and-millwrights.htm describes factory equipment maintenance occupations. This provides work context, not an individual company's buying intent or contract value.

## Example decisions
Agency acquisition: maintenance-company owner or sales leader. Client delivery: plant manager at a manufacturer. These are different campaigns.

Hypotheses to investigate: new manufacturing capacity, equipment changes and a need for outside maintenance support. Each needs company-specific evidence and a date before becoming a targeting trigger. None is claimed as a verified event in this fixture.

## Still unknown
Ten sourced buyer quotations, a verified company-level trigger, actual account value, actual sales cycle, capacity, reachable market size and buyer data. Obtain these in research and discovery. Do not invent them to satisfy a quota. The .example company evidence used by the executable demo is synthetic and separate from these real public sources.
