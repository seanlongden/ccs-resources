# Completed Fit Rules
Pack: 0.2.0-preview. Version 1. Context: agency-acquisition.

GOOD requires company evidence of industrial maintenance services sold to manufacturers, an Ohio service area, and an owner/managing director/sales leader contact. Source URL and evidence text required.
BAD: staffing-only, recruitment, agencies, consumer-only service, public-procurement-only work or clear geography mismatch.
MAYBE: missing/blocked website, unclear business, unknown sales cycle, unknown capacity, gateway/unknown mail route or missing role evidence. Hold for review.
Broad research estimates: label them ESTIMATED, do not pass them to final pricing as facts. Discovery confirms customer value, capacity and an actual short-enough sales process.
Human calibration: 50 synthetic rows, 30 GOOD, 10 BAD, 10 MAYBE. This tests the file process only. Real-company/LLM calibration is NOT TESTED.
Company name, size or a keyword alone does not establish ability to buy.
Rule owner: illustrative agency owner. No real contact approved.
