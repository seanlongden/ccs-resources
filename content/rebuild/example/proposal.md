# Completed Proposal Example
Pack: 0.2.0-preview. A constructed example with complete illustrative terms, not a live agreement.

## Abstract
Example Industrial Maintenance wants more suitable manufacturing work and has capacity for two more accounts (D1-D2). We propose a 90-day cold-email engagement to find and qualify needs reviews with Ohio manufacturing decision-makers. The target is ten accepted opportunities, for a $6,000 service fee split at the milestones below (D5-D6).

## Your Situation
Your sales path runs from first discussion through needs/site review, quotation and decision. You described a typical 30-60-day cycle. Your example first-year customer revenue is $60,000; gross profit and historic close rate are not known (D3-D4). We will not treat these revenue figures as profit or forecast when cash arrives.

## What We Will Do
Research suitable manufacturing sites, prepare and check the contact list, write the campaign, obtain your approval, operate the agreed sending route, qualify interested replies and report accepted needs reviews (D5). We do not perform engineering, issue maintenance quotations or close the final service sale.

## The Opportunity We Will Count
The site is a manufacturer in Ohio. A plant or maintenance decision-maker confirms an identified need within 90 days, ability to commission the work and a meaningful requirement within your capabilities, then agrees to a needs review. Existing opportunities, duplicates, vendors and jobseekers do not count (D7).

Each submitted opportunity includes reply and meeting evidence. You accept it or identify a failed criterion within two business days. Disputed rows remain pending and cannot trigger the second invoice. Silence does not count as acceptance (D10).

## Price And Protection
The term is 90 days from the agreed first sending day. The service fee is $6,000: $3,000 before build and $3,000 only after five accepted opportunities. There is no automatic renewal. Applicable tax and separately approved software/data charges are additional (D6,D11).

If no accepted opportunity is produced within the first 45 sending days, we refund the initial $3,000 within ten business days. You provide accurate inputs, approve materials, attend reviews and return feedback within two business days. A client-caused pause is recorded in writing and pauses the clock. The quarter's ten-opportunity target is distinct from this minimum and remedy (D9).

## Your Part
Nominate the sales owner, supply accurate proof and exclusions, approve the material, attend suitable meetings and provide timely feedback. Your team performs assessment, quotation, closing and maintenance delivery (D2,D5).

## Decision And Next Step
Review this scope with the operations manager and budget owner on the second call. Correct any mismatch, confirm exact dates and parties, then prepare the agreement and first invoice from the accepted terms. No real signature or payment is requested by this teaching document.

## Note To Decision-Makers
This engagement creates and operates a defined route to relevant Ohio manufacturing buyers. The agency handles research, list preparation, copy and reply qualification. Your team retains the technical sale and delivery. The $6,000 fee is split between build and five accepted opportunities, with the initial payment refunded if the stated 45-day minimum is missed under the participation terms. The example contains no past-result claim.
