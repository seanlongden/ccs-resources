# Illustrative proposal draft
Pack: 0.1.0-preview. Prepared from the accompanying exercise notes. Not ready to send.

## Abstract
Example Industrial Maintenance wants more suitable manufacturing work and says it has capacity to deliver (D1-D2). The illustrative engagement prepares and runs a cold-email acquisition process over 90 days for a total $6,000, using the exercise scope below (D5-D6).

## Situation and route
Your current process moves from an initial conversation to a needs/site review, scoped quotation and customer decision (D4). We propose researching appropriate manufacturing companies in the agreed region, checking company and contact fit, writing approved campaigns, qualifying responses and reporting opportunities (D5). We have not verified a market count or historical conversion rate.

## What counts and who does what
The final acceptance point must be agreed within your actual sales process (D7). The draft uses clear interest, financial ability, meaningful requirement and region/service fit as conditions. You confirm acceptance, attend sales discussions, provide accurate feedback and perform the maintenance. We do not perform engineering or guarantee your final close (D5).

## Budget and scope
Exercise inputs: 90 days, ten accepted opportunities, $6,000 total. $3,000 at start and $3,000 after five accepted opportunities (D6). These are illustrative inputs for the drafting exercise. No actual launch, invoice or signature is authorised.

## Economics and evidence
The assumed customer value is $60,000 first-year revenue (D3), not gross profit. No close rate is known, so this proposal does not project customer revenue or claim a 20x return. There are no approved past results (D8).

## Before final use
Confirm parties, region, exact opportunity acceptance/evidence, capacity, sales cycle, minimum/remedy, tax, dates and decision-makers (D2, D4, D7). Then review the actual scope and terms together.

## Note to decision-makers
This proposed project creates and operates a defined outbound route to relevant manufacturing buyers. The maintenance company retains final sales and service delivery. The budget and milestone split above are exercise terms. Acceptance and protection terms remain open, so this draft is for review only.
