# Illustrative offer
Pack: 0.1.0-preview. Teaching example, not a current CCS commercial promise.

We build and run an outbound campaign introducing established maintenance companies to manufacturing buyers in their service region.

Term: 90 days. Example fee: $6,000, $3,000 to start and $3,000 after five accepted qualified opportunities. Scope example: ten opportunities. These quantities require a delivery assessment for a real engagement.

Qualified opportunity: a relevant manufacturing decision-maker has expressed interest, confirms a need the maintenance firm serves, has a meaningful potential requirement the client agrees is worth pursuing, and is inside the service region. Acceptance evidence and the internal sales milestone must be written into a real agreement after discovery. A booked call alone does not automatically count.

Owner jobs: approve buyer rules and copy; take sales calls; collect accurate process and customer value data. Client jobs: confirm fit and capacity, attend meetings, give prompt opportunity feedback, perform maintenance services.

No invented guarantee: the real minimum, clock and remedy remain pending. Do not send this illustrative offer as an agreed proposal.
