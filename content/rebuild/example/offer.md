# Completed Offer
Pack: 1.0.0. Illustrative commercial design for teaching.

We build and run a cold-email campaign that introduces an Ohio maintenance company to manufacturing sites with a maintenance need worth discussing.

## Work And Result
Research the agreed market, check accounts and contacts, write two approved cold emails, load a checked campaign, qualify interested replies and report accepted needs reviews. The quarter's target is ten accepted opportunities. It is a target, not a promise of ten closed customers.

## Terms
90 days from the agreed first sending day. $6,000 service fee: $3,000 before build and $3,000 after five accepted opportunities. No automatic renewal. Applicable tax and an approved software/data budget are separate.

## Accepted Opportunity
An Ohio manufacturing site with a relevant plant or maintenance decision-maker; an identified need within 90 days; ability to commission work; a meaningful requirement the client confirms it can serve; and agreement to a needs review. Exclude duplicates, existing opportunities, vendors and jobseekers. A booked meeting alone does not count.

The client checks reply and meeting evidence within two business days. A disputed opportunity stays pending until reviewed together. Silence is not acceptance.

## Minimum And Remedy
At least one accepted opportunity in the first 45 sending days, or the initial $3,000 is refunded within ten business days. The client supplies accurate information, approves materials and attends/returns feedback within two business days. A client-caused pause is recorded and pauses the clock. The later $3,000 is not due until five are accepted.

## Responsibilities
Agency: research, lists, copy, approved campaign operation, reply qualification and reporting.
Client: correct inputs and proof, service capacity, feedback, needs reviews, technical quotation, final sale and maintenance delivery.
No guarantee of final sales or buyer profit is made. No real proposal or sending is authorised by this example.
