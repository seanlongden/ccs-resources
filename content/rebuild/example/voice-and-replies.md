# Completed Reply Practice
Pack: 0.2.0-preview. Written examples, not actual client messages.
Voice: brief, helpful, direct, no invented rapport or pressure.
Positive: "Happy to share the outline. We would research the agreed market, check fit and agree what counts before launch. Would Tuesday at 14:00 Europe/London work?" Times here are fixture data only.
Price: "The example quarter is $6,000, split into $3,000 before build and $3,000 after five accepted opportunities. We would confirm fit and scope before sending terms."
Opt-out: do not draft a new sales message; report STOP and the suppression action needed. Any actual account change is separately approved.
Referral: hold the new recipient until identity, role and permission are reviewed.
Unavailable slot: say the previously offered time is no longer available and draft a request to choose a genuinely available alternative. Never book a nearby time.
Each reply card includes latest thread version, recipient, sender, context, evidence, fit label, exact draft and actions requested. No send or booking is authorised.
