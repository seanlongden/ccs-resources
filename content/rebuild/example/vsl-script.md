# Completed Spoken Script Example
Pack: 1.0.0. Illustrative, not recorded or published.

If you run an industrial maintenance company in Ohio and have room for more manufacturing work, this is the process we would discuss with you.

Your team can perform the maintenance. The question is how to find suitable sites and reach the person who can discuss the need.

We would first agree the region, capabilities, capacity and what makes a needs review worth your time. Then we would research suitable manufacturing accounts, check the people, write the outreach and get your approval before it runs.

When someone replies, we would check the company and the actual need. A calendar booking by itself would not count as an accepted opportunity. The definition and evidence would be in the scope.

Your team would still assess the work, issue the quotation and close the sale. We would handle the agreed acquisition work, not your engineering or maintenance delivery.

There is no past-result claim in this example. On a discussion we can show the research, list checks and opportunity definition so you can judge whether the process fits.

The example engagement is a 90-day project with specific payment milestones and a minimum/refund condition. Read the full proposal for the terms rather than treating a target as a guarantee of sales.

If that sounds relevant, use the approved booking link on this page to discuss your market, capacity and current sales process. We will decide whether a scope makes sense for both sides.
