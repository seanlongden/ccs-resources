# Completed Discovery Notes
Pack: 1.0.0. A constructed teaching case, not a real call or member result.

D1. Example Industrial Maintenance sells preventive maintenance and equipment repair to manufacturers in Ohio. Its operations manager attends discovery.
D2. The exercise assumes capacity for two additional maintenance accounts this quarter and a sales owner able to attend two first meetings per week.
D3. Exercise customer value is $60,000 first-year revenue. Gross profit is UNKNOWN. No profit or cash timing is inferred from revenue.
D4. Exercise process: first discussion, needs/site review, scoped quotation, customer decision. The example buyer reports a typical 30-60-day cycle after first discussion. Historical close rate remains UNKNOWN.
D5. Agency work: research Ohio manufacturing accounts, prepare checked lists, write approved cold emails, operate the campaign, qualify responses and report accepted needs reviews. Client work: technical assessment, quotation, final sale and maintenance delivery.
D6. Illustrative commercial design: 90 days from the agreed sending start; target ten accepted opportunities; $6,000 total; $3,000 before build; $3,000 after five accepted opportunities. No automatic renewal.
D7. Acceptance: a manufacturing site in Ohio, a relevant plant/maintenance decision-maker, an identified maintenance need within 90 days, ability to commission work, meaningful scope confirmed by the client, and agreement to a needs review. Exclude jobseekers, vendors, existing opportunities and duplicates. A calendar booking alone does not count.
D8. No approved historical case studies. The agency must not claim it has produced $120,000.
D9. Example protection: if no opportunity meeting D7 is accepted within 45 days of sending, refund the initial $3,000 within ten business days. Student/client must approve materials, provide accurate inputs and attend/return feedback within two business days. Pauses caused by unavailable client inputs stop the clock, with both parties informed in writing. This is a designed example, not legal advice or current CCS terms.
D10. Opportunity review: submit supporting reply and meeting notes. Client accepts or explains a failed criterion within two business days. No automatic acceptance by silence. Disputed rows stay pending, do not trigger the milestone invoice, and are reviewed together.
D11. In this exercise tax is excluded from the $6,000 service fee and is added only where applicable. Software/data costs require a separate written budget. No invoice, signature or real transaction is authorised.
