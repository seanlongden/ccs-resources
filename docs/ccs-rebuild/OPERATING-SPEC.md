## Rules reconciled for the preview
- Exactly two cold-email steps; canonical first_name token, explicit provider mapping. One approved bump per recipient in the same thread. Sequence delay requires owner approval; propose three business days only as a draft.
- Interested-reply follow-up is a different workflow. Draft schedule follows the newer Launch Runbook and DWY pack: about 24 hours, then 1 day, then 2 days. Stop after the third. No automatic sends. The older two-bump guide is superseded in this preview proposal; Matt must approve before release.
- BAD: no draft. UNSURE: show the research doubt and a draft for review, never send. MAYBE company qualification does not mean permission to upload; owner approves that audience explicitly.
- Broad research screening may use a 120-day ceiling; the quarterly proposal uses the tighter under-60-day preference from G1. These are different stages, not contradictory universal thresholds. Unknown economics require discovery, not fabricated estimates.
- Revenue, stated-period customer value, LTV, gross profit, contracted fees and collected cash are separate. Do not infer margin from LTV. No guaranteed buyer ROI is implied by arithmetic.
- Lead runway = uncontacted eligible NEW contacts / daily NEW contacts. Total sends include bumps and must not be the denominator.
- Open and link tracking off for this proposed plain-text path. Exact provider settings and sender readiness must be checked on the selected account before loading.
- Blocklist UNKNOWN is not clean. MX addresses do not establish outbound sending IPs. Do not activate or alter DNS in a health check.
- Never replace an accepted calendar time with a nearby free time. Recheck both calendar and scheduler, duration, timezone and holds. Exact email match, not company-domain match alone, establishes a booking.
- Auto-send after an unanswered card is removed. No sending, booking, external write or paid operation is enabled in the preview pack.
- LinkedIn stays optional/manual. Conflicting 30/60-day activity windows remain a draft owner-selected setting, not a hidden default.

## Demonstration and handover
Local fixture chain: research source-status, profile/offer, 50 synthetic company labels, cleaned/qualified leads, locked copy, simulated paused campaign, draft/approval/booking simulation, weekly report and completed simulated handover. This demonstrates file contracts and safety logic only. It does not demonstrate AI accuracy, real web research, a working Grok skill install, API integration, delivery, customer conversion or profitability.
Real handover remains blocked until the actual tools produce a correctly qualified booking with working monitoring and no critical setup failure, reviewed by CCS. One booking is not repeatability.
